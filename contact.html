Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find immediate answers regarding stainless steel purity, customized wedding trousseaus, induction compatibility, and nationwide delivery.

01 What grade of stainless steel is used in your cookware and kitchenware?
All our cookware, dinner thalis, and food storage systems are strictly fabricated from premium food-grade 304 (18/8) stainless steel. This non-reactive alloy prevents acidic discoloration from tamarind, tomatoes, or curd, resists pitting and corrosion, and ensures 100% toxin-free everyday home cooking.
02 Can I inspect and customize a wedding utensil trousseau (Shagun Set) in person?
Yes, absolutely! We welcome couples and families to our Mumbai showroom for dedicated bridal consultations. You can examine metal gauge weights, compare handi designs, configure custom 51 to 101-piece setups, and personalize each vessel with bride and groom laser name engraving.
03 Are your stainless steel pots and pans compatible with induction cooktops?
Yes. All our tri-ply and encapsulated sandwich-bottom cookware series are engineered with an impact-bonded magnetic grade 430 exterior base. They perform flawlessly on induction hobs, traditional gas stoves, radiant electric cooktops, and commercial ceramic burners.
04 What is your delivery timeframe for bulk, restaurant, and pan-India orders?
Standard retail orders are dispatched within 24–48 hours and delivered in 3–5 business days nationwide. Bespoke wedding trunks, corporate gift boxes, and bulk hospitality orders are safely crated with multi-layer protective packaging and delivered within 7–10 business days across India.
05 What warranty and after-sales support do you provide on your products?
Every cookware piece is protected by our direct 5-Year Rust & Base Bond Warranty. Our dinner sets and organizers carry a lifetime food-grade purity guarantee against pitting, rusting, or weld failures under normal kitchen use. Replacement handles, lids, and steam whistles are readily available.
